About LUNARO

LUNARO is a Telegram-focused Web3 ecosystem designed around LNR mining, wallet connectivity, rewards, referrals and automated withdrawals on TON.

LUNARO Ecosystem

LUNARO combines a Telegram Mini App with the LNR token ecosystem, allowing users to connect their TON wallet, mine LNR, claim rewards and manage their assets.
